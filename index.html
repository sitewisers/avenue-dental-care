Helpful things to know

Appointment reminders

We send email and SMS reminders two working days before your appointment, so it’s easy to keep track of your visits.

Dental emergencies

Home/Treatments/Short-Term Orthodontics

Short-Term Orthodontics

Straighten your front teeth quickly, discreetly and comfortably — a great way to be smile-ready for a special occasion.

Free consultation

Discreet, fast tooth alignment

Short-term orthodontics focuses on aligning only the teeth you see when you smile, which means treatment is faster and more affordable than traditional braces. Dr Lackova will assess your suitability and give you an individual treatment timeline.

  • Clear brackets & tooth-coloured wires for a discreet look
  • Low, gentle forces for greater comfort
  • Most cases complete in around 6–18 weeks

Anterior Alignment Orthodontics (AAO)

A modified brace system with incredibly fast treatment times — a great way to straighten your teeth for a special occasion. Clear brackets and tooth-coloured wires use low force to move the teeth gently, making treatment more comfortable, while focusing on aligning only the teeth seen when you smile.

Inman Aligner

A simple removable appliance used to align front teeth quickly and safely. Nickel-titanium coil springs gently oppose each other, guiding the teeth into their new position. Worn 16–20 hours a day, most cases complete within 6–18 weeks.

Your journey

What to expect

1. Free consultation

We assess whether short-term orthodontics is right for you.

2. Impressions

Custom impressions are taken and your appliance is made.

3. Fitting & check-ups

Initial fitting, then check-ups every 2–3 weeks as your teeth align.

4. Retention

Lifetime retention with a bonded or Essix retainer keeps your smile in place.

Guide prices: Inman Aligner from £1,899 · ClearSmile Brace (dual arch) from £2,950. You’ll receive a written treatment plan after your consultation.

Botulinum Toxin (BTX)

Used to improve frown lines, crow’s feet and forehead lines, as well as for facial revitalisation. A purified protein is injected to block the transmission of nerve impulses to the facial muscles, softening wrinkles for a smoother, fresher appearance.

From £150

Dermal Fillers (Restylane)

Used to address nasolabial folds, lip contour and facial revitalisation, as well as décolletage and hand revitalisation. A crystal-clear gel form of hyaluronic acid hydrates the skin and adds natural-looking volume.

From £285

Spread the cost of your routine dental care and enjoy member discounts — from just £18.50 a month.

Membership made simple

Everything you need for healthy routine care

Our Practice Plan makes it easy to stay on top of your dental health with a simple monthly Direct Debit. It covers your routine examinations and hygiene visits, and unlocks member discounts on further treatment — plus worldwide emergency cover.

  • 2 routine 10-minute examination appointments, including clinical examination, screening for oral cancer and routine intraoral x-rays (usually a set of 2) where clinically necessary
  • 2 × 20-minute hygiene appointments
  • Oral hygiene instruction and preventative guidance
  • Complete treatment planning
  • 20% off assessment of emergencies and dental pain, with any temporary treatment required
  • 20% discount on additional hygiene visits
  • 20% discount on restorative work (fillings, root canal treatments, desensitising treatments)
  • 20% discount on surgical procedures including extractions (exclusions apply) and soft tissue surgery
  • 10% discount on laboratory-based prosthetic work (crowns, bridges, dentures, mouth guards)
  • Worldwide Accident & Emergency insurance
